Log symptoms on period days

* Build one symptom list for everyone you track. Nothing is preset, so add the ones that matter.
* On each period day, tap a symptom for mild, again for medium, then strong, then off.
* Manage the list under Settings, Cycle tracking, Symptoms, or from Edit symptoms on a period.
* Symptoms go with their period, ride in the encrypted backup, and Delete cycle data clears them.
